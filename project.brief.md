# My project brief

Which wards in Surulere LGA, Lagos State, are more than 5 km from a health facility?

## Why it matters

People living farther from health facilities may face greater difficulty accessing healthcare. Identifying wards with poor geographic access can help health planners understand where additional investigation or healthcare resources may be needed.

## The data needed
- Surulere LGA boundary
- Surulere ward boundaries 
- Health facility locations 
- Road network

## Data sources: 

- LGA boundaries - GRID3 -  GRID3 NGA – Operational LGA Boundaries (December 2020_updated September 2025) - URL: https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-operational-lga-boundaries/about
- Ward boundaries - GRID3 NGA – Operational Wards v1.0 (December 2020_updated September 2025) - URL: https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-operational-wards-v1-0/about
- Health facilities - GRID3 NGA - Health Facilities v2.0 (November 2024) - URL: https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-health-facilities-v2-0/about
- Road network - GRID3 NGA - Roads v1.0 (August 2025_updated September 2025) - URL: https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-roads-v1-0/about

## What is built: An interactive map of Surulere showing each ward, health facility locations and areas more than 5 km from the nearest facility, with a summary of potentially underserved wards.
